# Bibly: biblioteca de ilustrações 3D (prompts para o ChatGPT)

## Como usar

1. Abra um **chat novo** no ChatGPT, anexe a sua selfie e cole a **mensagem de abertura**.
2. Aprove a folha de personagem. Se não ficar parecida com você, peça ajustes antes de continuar.
3. Continue **no mesmo chat**: em cada mensagem, cole a **frase fixa** e, logo abaixo, a linha de **um** exercício.
4. Salve cada imagem com o nome do arquivo que aparece no título (ex.: `pernas-leg-press-45.png`).
5. Se alguma imagem sair diferente, responda: *"Keep her identical to the approved character sheet."*
6. Quando o chat ficar lento, abra outro, anexe a **folha de personagem aprovada** (em vez da selfie) e cole a abertura de novo.

O ChatGPT limita quantas imagens dá para gerar por dia. Comece pela seção **Prioridade** (os exercícios dos seus treinos atuais) e vá completando o resto aos poucos.

## Mensagem de abertura (cole uma vez, com a selfie anexada)

```
I'm creating a large library of 3D illustrations for my women's workout app "Bibly". Every image must show the SAME character, in the SAME style, lighting and camera, as if they were all from one illustration pack.

STYLE
- Soft 3D clay / Pixar-like render, rounded shapes, smooth matte materials, cute but realistic proportions.
- Soft diffused studio lighting from the upper left, gentle contact shadow on the floor.
- Transparent background (PNG). If transparency isn't possible, use a plain #FBF3F0 background.
- Square 1:1, full body visible and centered, with some margin around her.
- Camera at a 3/4 side angle (30–45°), at hip height, so the movement is clearly readable (unless a specific view is requested).
- No text, no logos, no brand names on any equipment.

CHARACTER (based on the attached photo: she must look like me)
- Young woman with light-medium warm olive/tan skin and dark brown eyes.
- Soft round face, full cheeks, gentle confident smile, glossy pink lips.
- Dark brown wavy/curly chin-length bob with middle-parted curtain bangs and volume at the sides. Hair always LOOSE, same hairstyle in every image.
- Thin oval gold metal-frame eyeglasses in EVERY image.
- Small gold hoop earrings and a very thin gold chain necklace.
- Real, curvy body with fuller hips and thighs, natural proportions. Not a skinny model, not exaggerated.
- Outfit, always identical: off-white cropped sports bra top (#FFFBF9), high-waisted dusty rose leggings (#D9867B).
- Footwear: white sneakers with dusty rose details for gym, cardio and functional exercises; BAREFOOT for yoga, pilates and stretching.

EQUIPMENT COLOR PALETTE (to match the app)
- Machine frames: warm cream/beige (#EADBD3), matte.
- Seat pads and grips: dusty rose (#E39A90).
- Cables and small details: rosy brown (#A8696A).
- Weight plates, barbells, dumbbells and kettlebells: muted rose-grey (#C9A9A3) with cream handles.
- Benches, boxes and exercise/yoga mat: light blush (#F6DFD9).
- No yellow, no black, no bright or saturated colors.

First, create a CHARACTER SHEET: her standing in front, side and back views, no equipment. I'll approve it before we move on.
```

## Frase fixa (cole no começo de toda mensagem)

```
Same character, outfit, style, lighting, camera and palette as the approved character sheet. Correct, safe form. Transparent PNG, square, full body.
```
